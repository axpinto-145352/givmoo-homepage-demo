What shoppers say

Questions

Do you offer bulk orders?

Yes. We offer bulk orders, decorated or blank in most cases. Our manufacturer and supplier network gives us millions of products to choose from. Request a quote or email hiya@givmoo.com to get started.

How long does shipping take?

Most products are made when you order them, which keeps prices low. Production takes 2 to 5 days and standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Personalized items may need 2 to 3 extra days. Shipping is calculated at checkout.

What is your return policy?

Because most products are made to order, they can only be returned if they arrive damaged or get lost. We'll also give you a one-time, no-questions-asked refund on an item that just didn't work out. Full policy

How does personalization work?

Select your product, choose the personalization option and enter your custom text, name or message. Our team makes your item and sends you a preview before it ships.
